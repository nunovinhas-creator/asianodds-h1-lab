# Competition identity: review package, not automatic certification

The productive index currently persists API-Football competition/team/fixture IDs, names and kickoff, but omits country and season. SportScore review rows contain labels and fixture URLs, not proven competition IDs. A label-only migration would invent evidence. No bindings are approved or activated in this PR; #159 remains blocked.

The offline registry keys full provider competition IDs plus explicit country, season, division, gender, age and squad. Unresolved international scope remains blocked rather than guessing a country. Source documents are pinned by SHA-256, require two independent official providers and at least two fixture witnesses plus separate official team-ID crosswalk evidence. Names and scores are not identity keys. Review-package validation is not proof of a document's authenticity: an explicit reviewer must check the captured documents and mapping provenance. Preparation only produces PROPOSED records; review produces REVIEWED_OFFLINE_NOT_ACTIVATED. Revisions are compare-and-swap and records immutable/conflict rejecting. Caller supplied IDs/flags are never consumed by productive certification.

Next prerequisites: capture country/season and official competition/team IDs from existing authorized normal responses without extra requests; retain original response digests and timestamps; obtain independent identity evidence for AsianOdds/source fixtures. Do not infer a binding from shared names, matching scores, fixture guesses, two copies of the same upstream source or manual approval without source evidence. Source independence itself needs review because vendors may resell the same feed. This framework does not claim universal competition identification.

Activation requires a separate PR and authorization, current fixture validation, same home/away order, kickoff, regulation FT and conflict quarantine. No H1, Telegram, strategy, quota, cooldown, workflow or production state change. Benefit now: a concrete fail-closed review contract with adversarial tests; zero newly proven productive identities or recovered FT claimed.
